Airbus A380-800 — The Giant

The world's largest passenger airliner: a full-length double-decker with two passenger decks and up to 853 seats. It entered service with Singapore Airlines in 2007 and became a symbol of scale, comfort and ambition. Production ended in 2021 after 251 aircraft, but its most famous liveries remain icons of modern aviation.
